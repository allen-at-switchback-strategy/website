BEFORE YOU'RE QUOTED

What can move the number

We look at all six before quoting, so the number in your scope is the number you pay. If none of them apply, the starting price is the price.

System complexity

How many systems the workflow touches, whether they have usable APIs, and whether anything needs a custom connector or a legacy workaround.

Organizational size

Headcount in scope, how many teams and locations, and how many stakeholder groups have to review before anything ships.

Condition of the data

Where it lives, how clean it is, and whether we have to stand up retrieval or a source of truth before the workflow can be trusted.

Governance & compliance

Regulated industries, security review, audit trails, procurement cycles, and legal sign-off on what a model is allowed to see.

Breadth of scope

Additional workflows, extra business units in the readiness score, more roles to train, or a second environment to support.

Timeline and onsite time

Compressed timelines that need parallel work, standing onsite days, or travel outside the Indianapolis metro.
