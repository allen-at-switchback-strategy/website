01 — PRINCIPLES
Signage, not decoration
Flat and legible
Solid fills, hard edges, real contrast. No gradients, no glassmorphism, no drop shadows deeper than 2px.
Graded, not hyped
Difficulty is stated plainly, the way a trail sign does it. Confidence comes from specificity, never from superlatives.
Momentum in the layout
Sequence and progress are visible: numbered stages, left-to-right rise, one clear next turn on every screen.
Red means go
Crimson is the summit marker — one use per view, on the single most important action or figure.
01B — LOGO
The mark
Nine circles on one baseline — three legs of two, three and four, each peaking one step higher than the last — then the summit star. Green is Green Trail, blue is Blue Trail, navy is Black Trail; the red star is the Pro-Line marker and the only red on any surface it appears on. Full specimen sheet, lockups, and swag variants live in Logo System.
PRIMARY · 2.03:1 · 46PX SCREEN / 72PX PRINT
COMPACT · 1:1 · 37PX SCREEN / 59PX PRINT
ON NAVY · PAPER CIRCLES · BLAZE LIGHT STAR
Never tighten the legs
Legs sit 40 units apart against 21 within a leg. Close that gap and the silhouette collapses into a sawtooth. Check every change in one color first.
Floors follow the circle
16px of circle for anything physical, 10px on screen. Below either floor, the star goes on alone — four elements will not survive an icon.
One red per surface
The star always wins the crimson. If a page already has a crimson CTA, the logo goes navy or paper on that page.

02B — CONTRAST RULES
Crimson never sits on a color
Crimson, blue, and green are all mid-lightness, so any two of them together fail WCAG AA. Crimson is legible on paper and on white only. On navy, green, or blue, swap in Blaze Light — the same hue lifted to 80% lightness. Never place a tier color on another tier color.
CRIMSON ON NAVY
AVOID · 2.6:1
Fails at every size.
BLAZE LIGHT ON NAVY
USE · 5.2:1
--sbs-red-light #EE7A82 for all accent text and small marks on navy.
CRIMSON ON GREEN
NEVER · 1.4:1
Also unreadable for red-green color deficiency. Separate them with paper or navy.
PAPER ON BLUE
USE · 6.8:1
On any tier color, text is paper or white — never another tier color.
--sbs-red:      #C8102E  · on paper / white only
--sbs-red-light: #EE7A82  · on navy — text & small marks
--sbs-green-light: #7FBF9F · success text on navy
--sbs-blue-light: #9FB0D0  · body text on navy
Targets: 4.5:1 for text under 24px, 3:1 for display figures and UI borders. Pure crimson stays legal as a fill behind white text (4.9:1) and as a 4px tier border, where it reads as a graphic rather than as type. Tier meaning always survives in shape and label, so a color that has to be lightened for contrast never costs meaning.

08 — ICONS, IMAGERY, MOTION
Supporting rules
Icons
Geometric line set on a 24px grid, 2px stroke, square caps, no fills. Use Lucide (MIT) as the base library and restrict it to navigation, status, and system affordances — the trail-grade shapes are the only brand marks. Never illustrative, never colored except by state.
Photography
SHOT LIST — see rules at right
Shoot four: a rider mid-switchback from below the turn; an empty bermed corner at dawn; hands on a bar-mounted map or GPS; Allen in a working session at a whiteboard. Cool morning light, desaturated greens, no lens flare, no stock-photo handshakes. Full-bleed at 16:9 or 3:4; text only over the navy 70% scrim.
Motion & space
--space: 4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 96 px
--radius: 0 (2px max in app)
--ease: cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)
--dur: 160ms UI / 280ms reveal
Motion moves up and to the right: 12px rise plus fade on reveal, never scale or bounce. Stage tracks fill left to right. One transition per interaction; respect reduced-motion by dropping to opacity only.
09 — SCALING TO THE CLIENT APP
What carries over in six months
The app is the same system at higher density: time tracking, booking, agent Q&A, and engagement progress. Notes for whoever builds it.
DENSITY
Body steps to 16px, labels to 11px, spacing scale drops one step. Radius may go to 2px; nothing rounder.
PROGRESS
The stage track is the app's home screen: switchbacks completed, current turn, what's next, hours logged against each.
AGENT VOICE
Agent messages sit on paper with a navy left rule; Allen's replies sit on white. Agent-suggested considerations are flagged with the blue square, never a sparkle.
TOUCH
44px minimum targets, thumb-reachable primary action, bottom tab bar in navy with crimson active marker.
